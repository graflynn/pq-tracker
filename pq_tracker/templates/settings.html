Two lists, both saved to topics.yaml:

  • Search terms — gate at ingestion. A PQ is only stored if at least one term appears in its text. Supports trailing-* wildcard (diabet* matches diabetes/diabetic/diabetics). Removing or narrowing this list does not retroactively delete rows; use the prune_corpus.py script for that.
